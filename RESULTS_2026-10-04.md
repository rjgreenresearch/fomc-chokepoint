# Results: In-Sample Test, Run of October 4, 2026

Data: data/incidents_full.csv (UKMTO WARNING series, coded per CODING_NOTES.md). Code and protocol are unchanged from the 11:02 AM build; MANIFEST.sha256 verifies.

## H1 primary
Eight-day windows ending on the five decision days held 18 attacks in 40 days. The other 177 days held 64. Rate ratio 1.245. Circular-shift permutation p = 0.277 (216 shifts). Binomial p = 0.243.

**Decision: fail to reject H0.** There is no detectable clustering of attacks before FOMC decisions.

## Per meeting (descriptive)
| Decision | Attacks in window | Prior 28-day pace per 8 days |
|---|---|---|
| March 18 | 7 | 3.1 |

The two high windows (March and September) match the attack pace of the month before them. The July window was quiet: the July cluster ran from July 13 to 20, ending two days before the window opened. In the three days before a decision, attacks ran at about a third of the normal rate.

## Bound for missing warnings
Treating all eight missing warning numbers as attacks and placing each inside a window wherever possible gives 22/40 vs. 68/177, RR 1.43, p = 0.161. The null holds.

## H2 (Brent)
Data: data/brent.csv (BZ=F daily closes, January 2 to October 2, 2026, 189 rows, fetched by the user with fetch_brent.py).
Mean pre-window log return +0.033. Per meeting: March +0.164, April +0.122, June -0.147, July -0.079, September +0.105. Circular-shift permutation p = 0.313 (216 shifts).

**Decision: fail to reject H0.** Two of five pre-decision windows saw Brent fall. The series contains daily moves above 7 percent on 14 days, most in March and April; the March 31 to April 1 drop of 15.7 percent may include a contract roll (Section 7 limitation).
